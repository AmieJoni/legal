Privacy Policy — Mijote

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Mijote is a local-first cooking assistant. It is designed so that your recipes, inventory, meal plans and family profiles stay on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device, and what never does.

Who we are

Mijote is published by Wordlye. For any privacy question, contact contact@wordlye.com.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no server-side profile.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDK, no tracking, no crash reporting.
  • Your content stays in a local database on your phone.
  • Optional AI features use your own API key and send requests directly from your device to the provider you choose.

Data stored on your device

Mijote stores the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Products, quantities, expiry dates and shopping lists.
  • Recipes, meal plans and cooking history.
  • Family member profiles (name, optional age band, dietary preferences and allergies) that you enter yourself.
  • App preferences: language, theme, notification settings.
  • Your AI provider configuration and API key, kept in the platform's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

We do not receive any of this data. It is protected by your device's operating-system sandbox and, when enabled, by your device encryption and screen lock.

AI features (bring your own key)

Mijote's "Chef IA" is optional. When you enable it and ask for a recipe or a suggestion, the app sends your request — the prompt, the relevant ingredients and the preferences needed to answer it — directly to the AI provider you configured (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or a custom compatible endpoint).

Those requests are governed by that provider's privacy policy and terms. Your API key is stored only on your device and is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Wordlye does not proxy, log or store AI requests, and does not receive your key.

If you do not configure an AI provider, Mijote works fully offline with its built-in local suggestions.

Backups and exports

You can export your data from the privacy settings. Exported backups are encrypted with a key you choose, and the file is written wherever you decide to save or share it. Wordlye never has access to that file or to its encryption key. Without the key, a backup cannot be restored — including by us.

Notifications

Expiry and meal reminders are scheduled locally by the operating system. No notification content is sent through our servers, because there are none.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about expiring products and planned meals.
  • Restart on boot: to reschedule those local reminders after a reboot.

Mijote does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. There is no third-party advertising or analytics processor, because the app contains none.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything from the app's privacy settings, or by uninstalling Mijote. Deleting the app removes the local database and the stored API key. Because we hold no copy, no additional deletion request is needed.

Children

Mijote is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Family profiles are intended to be created by an adult for the household.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Since Wordlye does not hold your data, you exercise these rights directly in the app: view, edit, export or erase your content at any time. You may still contact us with any question or complaint, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date, and material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.
