Privacy Policy — Vivex

Last updated: March 2026

Summary

Vivex is a 100% offline first-aid guide. We collect no data. The app has no internet connection.

Data Collection

  • No internet — Works entirely offline. No data transmitted.
  • No analytics — No tracking, no crash reporting.
  • No accounts — No sign-up, no login.
  • No location, camera, or microphone

Local Storage

Your preferences (language, favorites) are stored locally via SharedPreferences. Deleted on uninstall.

Medical Disclaimer

Vivex provides general first-aid guidance for educational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional medical care. Always call emergency services when available.
