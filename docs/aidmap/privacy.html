Privacy Policy — Aidmap

Last updated: March 2026

Summary

Aidmap is an offline-first crisis resource mapping app. Your location data stays on your device.

Data Collection

  • Location — Used locally to show nearby resources on the map. Never transmitted to any server unless you explicitly submit a resource report.
  • Resource reports — If you report a resource (water, shelter, medical), the blurred GPS coordinates and resource type are sent to our API. Your identity is never attached.
  • No accounts — No sign-up, no login, no user profiles.
  • No analytics — No tracking, no telemetry.

Data We Receive

When you submit a resource report: blurred GPS location (±500m), resource type, quantity, and timestamp. No device ID, no IP logging, no personal information.

Local Storage

Map data and preferences are cached locally on your device for offline use.

Third-Party Services

Map tiles may be fetched from OpenStreetMap when online. No other third-party services.
