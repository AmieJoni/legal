Terms of Use — Telox

Last updated: March 2026

Purpose

Telox enables peer-to-peer communication without internet infrastructure. It is designed for scenarios where traditional networks are unavailable.

No Warranty

Message delivery depends on physical proximity of mesh nodes. The app is provided "as is" with no guarantee of message delivery or timing.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content of your messages. Do not use Telox for illegal activities.

Encryption

E2EE protects message content. However, metadata (sender/recipient IDs, timestamps) is visible to relay nodes.
