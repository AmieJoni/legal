Privacy Policy — Telox

Last updated: March 2026

Summary

Telox is a serverless mesh messaging app. There are no servers. Messages travel directly between devices via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct.

Data Collection

  • No servers — Messages are transmitted peer-to-peer. We never see them.
  • No accounts — Your identity is a locally-generated cryptographic key pair.
  • No analytics — No tracking, no telemetry.
  • No internet required — Works entirely via BLE and Wi-Fi Direct mesh.

Encryption

All messages are encrypted end-to-end (E2EE) using X25519 key exchange + ChaCha20-Poly1305. Only the recipient can decrypt.

Local Storage

Messages and keys are stored encrypted on your device using Hive (AES-256). Deleted when you uninstall.

Mesh Relay

Messages may be relayed through other Telox devices to reach their destination (flood routing). Relay nodes cannot read the content — only encrypted packets pass through.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth — Required for BLE mesh communication
  • Location — Required by Android for BLE scanning (we do not use or store location)
  • Nearby Devices — Required for Wi-Fi Direct peer discovery
