Privacy Policy — Okayo

Last updated: March 2026

Summary

Okayo is a 100% offline crisis lifeline app. All data is encrypted and stored only on your device.

Data Collection

  • No internet — The app works entirely offline. No data is ever sent to any server.
  • No analytics — No tracking, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • No accounts — Protected by a local PIN only.

What the App Stores Locally

  • Emergency contacts — Encrypted with AES-256 on your device.
  • GPS position — Cached locally for SMS inclusion. Never transmitted to any server.
  • PIN hash — Stored locally for app access control.
  • Dead Man's Switch timer — Stored locally.

SMS

When triggered (SOS, Dead Man's Switch, battery critical), the app sends SMS via your phone's native SMS to your configured contacts. The SMS contains your GPS position. This uses your carrier's SMS network, not our servers.

Panic Wipe

The panic wipe feature permanently deletes all app data from your device. This action is irreversible.
