Privacy Policy — Haveno

Last updated: March 2026

Summary

Haveno is a 100% offline panic stabilization app. We collect no data. We have no servers. We cannot see anything you do.

Data Collection

Haveno does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data:

  • No internet connection — works entirely offline
  • No analytics — no tracking, no crash reporting
  • No accounts — no sign-up, no login
  • No location data — no GPS access
  • No camera or microphone

Local Storage

Your preferences (language, brightness, volume) are stored locally on your device only via SharedPreferences. Deleted when you uninstall.

Trusted Contact

If you save a trusted contact number, it stays on your device only. SMS is sent via your phone's native app.

Third-Party Services

None. No Firebase, no analytics, no ads, no cloud.
