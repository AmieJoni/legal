Privacy Policy — BarberAI

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

BarberAI is a barbershop management platform. We collect minimal data to provide booking, authentication, and AI try-on features.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — If you sign up (email, Google, or Apple), we store your name and email for authentication via Firebase.
  • Profile data — Barbers may provide a business name, address, services, pricing, and photos. This is stored in Firebase and displayed publicly.
  • Appointments — Booking dates, times, and barber preferences are stored temporarily for scheduling.
  • Location — Used with your permission to show nearby barbershops on the map. Not stored on servers.
  • Camera — Used for AI virtual try-on (ML Kit face mesh). All face processing happens on your device. No images are uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
  • Google AdMob — Advertising (optional, only in free tier). Standard AdMob identifier may be used.
  • Google ML Kit — Face mesh detection for virtual try-on (on-device, no data leaves your phone)
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — Optional social authentication

Data Retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Appointment data is retained for 12 months after the booking date. You can request data deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. You can also disable AdMob personalization in your device settings.
